(aparecem automaticamente; as que faltarem sao ignoradas):

  /background/salvador/       01.webp ... 06.webp

  /background/fortaleza/      01.webp ... 05.webp
  /background/sao-paulo/      01.webp ... 05.webp

  src/components/Home/BackgroundGlobe.tsx (const CITIES)
