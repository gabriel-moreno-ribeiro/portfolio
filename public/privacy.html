Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what data gabrielmr.com collects and how it's used. It's short and honest.

Camera & Gesture Mode

The site has an optional hands-free navigation mode powered by MediaPipe (Google). To use it, you must explicitly click "Enable Handsfree Mode" and grant camera permission.

  • Camera data never leaves your device.
  • All processing happens locally in your browser — no frames are sent to any server.
  • No images or video are stored or recorded.

You can revoke camera access at any time from your browser settings, or by refreshing the page without clicking the button.

Analytics

This site uses PostHog for privacy-respecting analytics. PostHog is configured with:

  • Session recordings — enabled, but all text inputs and content are masked. No keystrokes or typed content are captured, including in the terminal.
  • Do Not Track — if your browser sends a DNT header, PostHog respects it and no data is collected.
  • Page views and page leaves — used to understand which sections of the portfolio are most visited.

No personal identifiers are collected. No data is sold to third parties.
