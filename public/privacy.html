Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what data gabrielmr.com collects and how it's used.

Camera & Gesture Mode

The site has an optional hands-free navigation mode powered by MediaPipe (Google). To use it, you must explicitly click "Enable Handsfree" and grant camera permission.

  • Camera data never leaves your device.
  • All processing happens locally in your browser, and no frames are sent to any server.
  • No images or video are stored or recorded.

You can revoke camera access at any time from your browser settings, or by refreshing the page without clicking the button.

Analytics

This site uses PostHog for privacy-respecting, cookieless analytics.

  • No cookies. PostHog runs in memory only. The site itself keeps two small preferences in your browser’s localStorage (light or dark theme, and whether you have seen the hands-free intro).
  • No session recordings. Screen captures, clicks and interactions are not recorded.
  • Do Not Track is respected. If your browser sends a DNT header, no data is collected at all.
  • What is collected: page URL, referrer, device type, browser and country (from IP, not stored), to see which sections are visited most.

No personal identifiers are collected, and nothing is tracked across sessions. No data is sold to third parties. Analytics data is hosted by PostHog (US servers).

Contact Form

If you submit the contact form at the bottom of the home page, the following data is collected:

  • Your name, to address you in a reply.
  • Your email, to reply to you.
  • Your message.

This data is used only to reply to your message and for any follow-up.

Your message reaches my inbox through Resend (an email delivery service based in the US). Resend processes the data only to deliver it and does not keep the message after delivery is confirmed.

Messages stay in my inbox only as long as needed to reply, and 12 months at most, then they are deleted.

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing the address below. I will respond within 15 business days.

Your Rights Under LGPD

Under Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether your data is being processed.
  • Access your personal data.
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Request anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data.
  • Request data portability.
  • Revoke consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email me at the address below.
